# REFAL Dashboard Admin Control Plan

This plan supplements `.planning/REFAL-BRAIN-MASTER-PLAN.md`; the master plan remains authoritative. It tracks the admin workspace work requested by the owner.

## Goal

Give Refalco administrators one clear, category-based dashboard for the existing Supabase-backed company brain and operational settings. Keep each record in its existing source-of-truth table, preserve review/audit gates, and verify that approved saved information reaches Rafa without redeploying the agent.

## Constraints

- Never duplicate company facts across a new CMS table or copy records into a second store just to present them together.
- Knowledge revisions stay pending until reviewed and indexed; facts retain evidence and reviewer history; typed commercial records retain effective dates and audited changes.
- Customer memories stay separate from company knowledge and are never promoted automatically.
- Do not expose a generic schema editor, executable hooks, secrets, or unrestricted prompt editing to dashboard users.
- Keep WhatsApp, dashboard, and Edge behavior rules aligned. Changes to prompt contracts require parity regressions.
- Preserve the user's dirty worktree and existing live appointment/customer data.
