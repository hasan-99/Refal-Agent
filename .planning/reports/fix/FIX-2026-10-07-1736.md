## Root Cause
- `src/bot.js:394` searched only the current message, so short follow-ups such as “كم التكلفة؟” lost the earlier company-formation subject.
- `dashboard/knowledge.js:208` produced chunks up to roughly 6,500 characters while `src/ai.js` supplies at most 2,400 characters of each chunk to the model, hiding relevant facts later in the chunk and weakening embeddings.
- `src/intent.js:45` did not recognize the natural Arabic definite form `كم التكلفة؟` as pricing, causing price evidence to be stripped before generation.
- `src/language.js:37` recognized explicit switch commands but not natural supported-language capability questions such as `بتحكي انكليزي؟`.
- `src/refalcoAnswer.js:11` validated an entire mixed price chunk rather than selecting its strongest approved price passage, so unrelated restricted content forced a safe abstention.

## Patch
- Files changed: `dashboard/knowledge.js`, `src/bot.js`, `src/intent.js`, `src/language.js`, `src/refalcoAnswer.js`.
- Added context-aware retrieval for short follow-ups and canonical business-area retrieval for broad service questions.
- Reduced future knowledge chunks to at most 1,800 characters and re-indexed all five approved documents into 28 bounded chunks with fresh embeddings.
- Expanded supported-language detection and Arabic pricing intent coverage.
- Made deterministic pricing select the strongest approved, current price passage instead of validating unrelated text from the full source chunk.
