# Debug Report — 2026-10-14 07:20 UTC

**Symptom:** The saved content panel did not scroll as expected on the Company brain page.
**Mode:** frontend
**Tool calls used:** 5 investigation calls

## Investigation

- Inspected the Knowledge page overflow rules and measured the live browser element dimensions.
- Checked the live revision list and content blocks after the user clarified the affected section.
- The Knowledge detail panel had no height cap or scroll container, so it expanded with its content instead of scrolling within the panel.

## Root Cause

`dashboard/src/redesign.css` — the Knowledge detail panel had no bounded height or vertical overflow, so the saved-content section itself could not scroll.

## Fix Applied

- Added a viewport-aware height cap and vertical scrolling to the Knowledge detail panel; revision text remains free of nested scrollbars.
- Kept the detail panel title visible while scrolling and allowed the panel to flow naturally on mobile.
- Removed the duplicate top-level Company brain heading while preserving the REFAL KNOWLEDGE AND COMPANY DATA intro card and its title.
- Verification: dashboard production build, dashboard tests, and live browser checks.
