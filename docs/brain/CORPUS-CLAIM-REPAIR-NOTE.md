Reviewed the English, Arabic and Greek documents for `permanent-residency`, `relocation-checklist`, and `banking-and-payment-gateways` against the owner manual, `SOURCE-ANALYSIS.md`, `src/factCatalogue.js`, and the canonical topic/fact map in `src/brainFactMap.js`. All existing required fact IDs remain present in each language. No fact-register row, approval status, or other topic was changed.

| Topic | Claim disposition across EN, AR and EL |
| --- | --- |
| Permanent residency | Kept only the €300,000 qualifying investment plus applicable VAT (MB-F30); outside-Cyprus annual income amounts (MB-F31–33); and the four investment categories (MB-F38–41), narrowed to current programme wording. | Banking and payment gateways | Kept the no-guarantee rule for accounts and named platforms, the institution's own risk and KYC/AML decision (MB-F28–29), and the manual's supported clean-file positioning and single discovery question.
