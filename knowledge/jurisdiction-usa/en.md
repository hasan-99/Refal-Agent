## What a comparison can establish
<!-- facts: MB-J4 -->
Whether a U.S. or Cyprus entity fits depends on the business, ownership, customers, and where the work is carried out. Company registration alone does not establish tax residence, determine VAT duties, or guarantee that a bank or payment provider will accept the business.

## What the Cyprus side adds

## What this comparison does not decide
<!-- kind: boundary; facts: MB-J0 -->
We compare jurisdictions neutrally and do not present a universal winner. The relevant choice depends on your business activity, owners’ tax residence, customer locations, operating plans, and applicable legal obligations. Which of these matters most in your case? Tax, immigration, banking, and regulatory conclusions require current evidence and appropriate specialist review.

## What we need to know to make it concrete
<!-- kind: discovery; facts: MB-J4 -->
Where are the customers, owners, and people carrying out the work?

How is money received, and has the bank or payment provider confirmed its requirements?

What elections has the LLC made, and what income does it expect? Those details help a U.S. tax adviser assess classification, sourcing, and filing duties.
