## Where the group comes from
<!-- facts: MB-C1, MB-C2 -->
Refalco Group has operational roots from the year 2000. That is when activity on the ground started, not the date of a website or a recent rebrand.

On top of that sits more than 20 years of field experience. Field experience means files carried to the end: companies formed, structures changed, property developed, investors taken through a process and the obligations that follow it.

So how long the group has been in the market is answered by two numbers rather than one. The year 2000 is a starting point. The 20 years is continuity, and continuity is the harder part, because a group can have an old founding date and no practice behind it.

A group that has been in the market that long has watched the market change more than once, and that history is why a first conversation here usually opens wider than a single registration form.

## What the project numbers count
<!-- facts: MB-C3, MB-C4 -->
Two numbers describe the delivery record, and each one counts something different.

**47 real estate development projects.** These are development projects in real estate: taking a project from plan through construction to delivery.

**More than 400 multi sector projects.** This is the wider count, across several sectors rather than one line of business. Company formation and corporate structuring, tax and accounting files, investment files and property all sit inside that number.

So the first number describes depth in one field, and the second describes breadth across fields. Neither is a forecast. Both are a record of work already delivered.

## Not a narrow company registration office
<!-- facts: MB-C5 -->
The positioning is deliberate. Refalco does not present itself as a narrow company registration office. It works as a gateway to comprehensive investment and structural solutions.

The services sit in a few connected groups rather than in one product. Company formation and corporate structuring. Tax and accounting services. Investment, property and development services, with the files that run alongside them. Residency and relocation services for the people behind the company. If what you are looking for is the registration on its own, that is available too, but it is the smallest of the services here. Those service lines do connect in practice, because a company formed here usually leads straight into the tax, accounting and residency work that follows it.

The practical difference is simple. A registration office hands over a certificate and the file closes. A gateway keeps the same file open for what comes after, because incorporation is one stage out of many and the questions that follow it are structural: where the real presence sits, how tax and accounting are run, whether property or residency belong in the plan, and what changes when a partner or an investor arrives.

So which of the services you need is usually decided by what the company is for. That is why the first question we ask is usually that one, rather than which name you want to reserve.

## What the track record does not tell you
<!-- kind: boundary -->
The dates and the project counts describe the group's history. They are not a prediction about any individual file or any individual outcome.

A long record does not approve anything. A bank account, a payment gateway, a residency application or a licence are decided by banks, by institutions and by the authorities. No track record makes those automatic, and nobody can promise them in advance.

The numbers also do not say which structure suits you, what your file will cost, or how long it will take. Those depend on the activity, on where the customers and the money are, and on what already exists.

And they do not answer who exactly will handle your file or in which language. That is confirmed by the team directly, not inferred from a history.

## What we need from you to be concrete
<!-- kind: discovery; facts: MB-C5 -->
Three short answers move the conversation from a profile to your actual file.

What is the activity? Trading goods, selling services, software and intellectual property, holding shares, or property and development.

Where are the customers and the money? Inside the EU, outside it, or both.

What is the goal behind the company? A European presence, a payment gateway, residency, an investment, or a structure that holds something you already own.

With those three we can say which part of the group's work is relevant to you and which specialist should take the next conversation.
