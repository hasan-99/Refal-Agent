## School options

For a relocating family, REFAL distinguishes public, private and international schools with British or English curricula. The children's ages help shortlist suitable schools and areas. How old are your children?

## Healthcare
<!-- facts: MB-F43 -->
Cyprus has a national health system called GESY. Access depends on beneficiary eligibility and registration requirements. The cited GESY guidance does not establish that private insurance can simply be added on top; check coverage separately with the insurer and a qualified adviser.

## Housing, transport and car costs
<!-- kind: boundary; facts: MB-F44 -->
Housing, transport and car purchase costs vary. REFAL does not give generic amounts for them; a fresh estimate should be based on the city and family size.
