## No one can promise approval
<!-- kind: boundary; facts: MB-F28, MB-F29 -->
No one can promise that a bank account will be opened or a payment gateway such as Stripe, PayPal, Amazon or Shopify will be activated. This is the golden rule for both account opening and payment gateway activation.

## The institution makes the decision
<!-- kind: boundary; facts: MB-F28, MB-F29 -->
The final decision belongs to the financial institution. It depends on that institution's own risk assessment and its own KYC and AML compliance requirements. REFAL cannot promise or replace the decision another institution makes.

## A useful next step
<!-- kind: boundary; facts: MB-F28, MB-F29 -->
REFAL can focus on building a clear company file from the start, but cannot promise another institution's decision. If your goal is to use a bank or payment gateway, what does your business do and where are your customers?
