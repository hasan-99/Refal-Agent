## Three different structures, not one scale
<!-- facts: MB-J3 -->
The cited Bulgarian government source states a 10% corporate tax rate. Malta's corporate tax rate is 35%, and eligible shareholders may claim refunds under its system subject to applicable conditions. These figures do not establish a business's total tax burden; confirm current treatment with local tax advisers.

## Why the rate is only one element of the comparison
<!-- facts: MB-J3 -->
A headline rate or refund mechanism does not establish the total tax cost for a particular business. The result depends on activity, ownership, residence, and distributions; obtain advice in each relevant jurisdiction.

## What we will not claim
<!-- kind: boundary; facts: MB-J0 -->
We compare jurisdictions neutrally and do not present a universal winner. The relevant choice depends on your business activity, owners’ tax residence, customer locations, operating plans, and applicable legal obligations. Which of these matters most in your case?
