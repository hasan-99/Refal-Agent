## The ten stages of the buyer journey
<!-- facts: MB-F45 -->
Buying property in Cyprus runs through ten stages, in this order.

1. **Search.** The budget, the city, the property type and the purpose are set, and a shortlist is drawn from what is actually available.
2. **Selection.** The shortlist narrows to one specific unit, with its floor, its orientation, its finish and its delivery status.
3. **Reservation Deposit.** The chosen unit is taken off the market while the paperwork starts. The terms belong to the project and the unit, not to a general rule.
4. **Legal Due Diligence.** An independent lawyer checks the title, the real owner, any loan or burden registered on the land, the permits and the standing of the seller or developer.
5. **Contract.** The sale contract is drafted and signed. It fixes the price, the specification, the payment schedule and the delivery obligations.
6. **Payment Plan.** Payments follow the schedule written into the contract. On a property under construction they are normally tied to building milestones.
7. **Tax and VAT.** The tax and VAT position of the specific property and the specific buyer is worked out before completion.
8. **Transfer and Registration.** The contract is lodged with the Land Registry and the title moves into the buyer's name.
9. **Delivery.** The unit is handed over, inspected and connected to utilities.
10. **Management.** After handover the property is maintained, let or managed, depending on what it was bought for.

Most enquiries arrive at stage one. The stages where money and risk actually concentrate are three through seven.

## Where the legal protection sits
<!-- facts: MB-F45 -->
Four of the ten stages carry the legal weight.

**Legal Due Diligence** is done by an independent lawyer, not by the seller. It covers the title deed, who legally owns the land, whether any mortgage or encumbrance is registered against it, and whether the planning and building permits are in place.

**Contract** turns the conversation into an obligation. The exact unit, the specification, the total price, the payment schedule, the delivery date and the remedies if it is missed all live here.

**Transfer and Registration** makes the buyer the registered owner. Lodging the signed contract with the Land Registry protects the buyer's position in the period before the title moves. Timing depends on the Land Registry and on the title status of the specific property, so it is confirmed per file rather than quoted as a general number.

Whether the purchase is made in a personal name or in a company name, and what a non resident buyer needs in place, is settled before the Contract stage. Both change the registration and the tax treatment, so they are decided early rather than discovered late.

## The reservation deposit is never a figure we quote in advance
<!-- kind: boundary; facts: MB-F50 -->
There is a reservation step in the journey. There is no standard amount attached to it.

The terms and the value of a reservation differ from one project to the next and from one unit to the next. They are set by the developer and by the specific property, which is why they are read from the live property records at the moment you ask rather than recalled from a previous file. Anything stated before that check would be a guess, and this is the one number where a guess costs a buyer real money and real trust.

So the sequence is fixed. You pick the unit. The current reservation terms for that unit are pulled from the live inventory. You then see the exact amount, the conditions attached to it, the time it holds the unit for, and what happens to it if the sale does not proceed, all in writing before anything is agreed.

What is refundable, and in which circumstances, works the same way. It is a contractual term of that specific project, not a market convention, and it is confirmed from the project's own terms.

## What we need from you to make the journey concrete
<!-- kind: discovery; facts: MB-F45 -->
Four answers turn the ten stages into a real shortlist.

What is the purpose? A home to live in, a property to let, or a purchase connected to a residency application. The purpose changes the city, the unit type and the tax position.

What is the budget, and is it ready or being raised? This sets the band, and it decides whether a completed unit or a staged payment plan fits better.

Which city, and why? Work, schools, airport access and lifestyle pull in different directions.

When do you need it? A buyer who needs keys this year and a buyer who can wait for a future delivery date are looking at two different parts of the market.

With those four, the matching units can be pulled from the live inventory and the stages that actually apply to you can be walked through one by one.
