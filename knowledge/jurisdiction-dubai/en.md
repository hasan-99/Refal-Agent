## Dubai and Cyprus answer different questions
<!-- facts: MB-J1 -->
The UAE government states that it does not levy personal income tax. This does not determine an individual’s tax position in another country. Cross-border tax treatment depends on residence, income, and activities; seek case-specific advice.

## A Cyprus company is not necessarily a replacement for Dubai
<!-- facts: MB-J1 -->
Whether a UAE or Cyprus entity suits a business depends on its activity, owners, customers, and operating locations. Registration alone does not resolve cross-border tax duties or guarantee bank or payment-provider acceptance.

## What we need to know before comparing anything
<!-- kind: discovery -->
Four short answers turn this from a general comparison into something that applies to you.

Where are your customers? Mostly EU, mostly Gulf, or spread across both.

Where does the banking and the money flow need to sit, and do you already have a working account somewhere.

What is the activity? Services, trading goods, e-commerce, software and intellectual property, or holding assets.

Do residency and property matter to you personally, or is this purely an operating company question.

With those four, the next conversation can be about your structure rather than about two countries in general.
