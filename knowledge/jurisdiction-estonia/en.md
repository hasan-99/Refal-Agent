## What Estonian e-Residency is, and what it is not
<!-- facts: MB-J2 -->
e-Residency is a digital identity and does not grant physical residence. Estonia generally taxes company profits when distributed, subject to exceptions and cross-border rules. The exact outcome depends on the company and owner circumstances; consult an Estonian tax adviser.

## Where the Cyprus environment is structurally different
<!-- facts: MB-J2 -->
e-Residency does not determine where a person is tax-resident or where a company is managed. A comparison with Cyprus depends on the founder’s residence, company operations, and intended profit distributions.

## What a country comparison cannot settle
<!-- kind: boundary; facts: MB-J0 -->
We compare jurisdictions neutrally and do not present a universal winner. The relevant choice depends on your business activity, owners’ tax residence, customer locations, operating plans, and applicable legal obligations. Which of these matters most in your case? ## What we need to know to compare properly
<!-- kind: discovery; facts: MB-J2 -->
Where are the owners and operations based? Is the founder planning to distribute profits or retain them in the company? These details help an Estonian and Cyprus tax adviser assess the specific case.
