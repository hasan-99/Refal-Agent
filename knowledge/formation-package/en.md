## The seven things the package covers
<!-- facts: MB-F2, MB-F3, MB-F4, MB-F5, MB-F6, MB-F7, MB-F8 -->
The formation package is a fixed bundle of seven items. This is exactly what is included, and the list does not change from one file to the next.

1. **Cyprus Ltd formation.** The full official procedure, usually about two weeks after the documents are complete. This is what creates the formal legal entity you can operate with inside the EU.
2. **Company name reservation and approval.** The name is filed and reserved with the Cyprus Registrar of Companies, which is where the commercial identity formally begins.
3. **Document preparation and attestation.** The Memorandum and Articles of Association are drafted and filed, so the file is correct and is not rejected on administrative grounds.
4. **Certificate of Incorporation.** The official company certificates are issued. This is the core document for opening accounts and signing contracts.
5. **Company Secretary.** Included for 4 consecutive months.
6. **Registered Address.** Included for 4 consecutive months.
7. **Remote file management.** The procedures are completed without you travelling in the early stages, which saves an international founder both time and cost.

## Company Secretary and Registered Address, what they actually are
<!-- facts: MB-F6, MB-F7 -->
Two of the seven items are read wrongly often enough to be worth stating plainly.

**Company Secretary** is a statutory corporate function that a Cyprus company is required to have. It is not a personal assistant. It covers the formal corporate role the law expects the company to fill, and it is included for 4 consecutive months.

**Registered Address** is the company's official address in Cyprus for receiving official correspondence. It is not a private office and not an apartment you can work from. It is included for 4 consecutive months.

If what you actually need is desk space, meeting rooms, mail handling or real commercial presence in Cyprus, that is a separate service and a separate conversation, not part of the registered address.

## Timing and how the file is run
<!-- facts: MB-F2, MB-F8 -->
Formation usually takes about two weeks after the documents are complete.

The wording matters. The clock runs from the moment the file is complete, not from the first message, so the part you control is how quickly the identification and signed documents come back.

The early stages are handled remotely. You do not need to travel to Cyprus for them, which is what makes the process workable when the founder is abroad.

Everything in those stages is exchanged electronically. You send us scans of the identification and the signed documents, we send back what has been filed, and the exchange runs on whichever channel you already use for messaging rather than one you have to adopt for us.

Name approval at the Registrar and government processing sit with the authorities. Those steps are tracked and chased, never promised with a fixed date.

## The price and any active offer are confirmed live
<!-- kind: boundary; facts: MB-F9 -->
The package has a headline price, and it is deliberately not written into this document. The current price and any active promotion are read from the live pricing table and confirmed with you before anything is agreed, so you are never working from a figure that has since moved.

Three things to know about it:

The price is quoted in euro and before VAT. VAT is added on top where it applies.

Government and third party fees are separate from the package and are confirmed from current figures rather than estimated.

The Company Secretary and the Registered Address are included for the first 4 consecutive months. After that they become an annual renewal, priced from the current renewal schedule.

Payment terms, including whether an instalment option is available for your case, are confirmed by the team for your specific file.

## Hidden costs, cheaper offers, and what you actually pay in total
<!-- kind: boundary; facts: MB-F9 -->
Three versions of the same question come up constantly, and each deserves a straight answer rather than a deflection.

Are there costs beyond the package? Yes, and they are named before you commit. Government and third party charges sit outside the package, VAT applies on top, and the Company Secretary and the Registered Address become an annual renewal once the included period ends. None of that is hidden; it is simply not part of the bundle.

If you have found a cheaper offer elsewhere, compare the two line by line rather than at the headline. A quote that looks more expensive often includes items the cheaper one bills separately later, and the total you pay across the first year is the only comparison that settles anything.

If the package feels expensive against what you expected, the productive conversation is which items you need now and which can wait, not a discount on a figure that is read live anyway.

## What we need from you to open the file
<!-- kind: discovery; facts: MB-F3 -->
Four short answers are enough to start.

The company name you want, ideally with a second and third choice. Name approval sits with the Registrar, so alternatives save a round trip.

The activity. What the company will actually do, in one or two lines, because it drives the objects in the constitutional documents.

Who the shareholders and the directors will be, and where each of them is resident.

Whether anything already exists elsewhere, such as a company abroad or a partner joining later. That changes which structure is being discussed before any document is drafted.
