## English — detailed Cyprus company registration and formation service

REFALCO's confirmed customer-facing offer is assistance with forming a Cyprus private company. The service covers Cyprus company registration, reserving and obtaining approval for a proposed company name, preparing and submitting the incorporation documents within the service scope, issuing the Certificate of Incorporation, and following the application through the incorporation process. Formation procedures can generally be handled remotely in suitable cases, so personal travel is normally not required for the incorporation itself. A later banking or third-party service may have separate requirements.

The usual process has four stages: share one or preferably more proposed company names; provide the basic documents and company information; REFALCO prepares and submits the incorporation file to the competent Cyprus authorities; then the company documents are issued after the authorities complete the process. The approved basic-document list is a passport, proof of address, proposed company name, details of partners or shareholders when applicable, and the nature of the business activity. Extra information may be required depending on the activity, ownership, nationality, compliance review, or authority requirements.

The expected formation time is approximately two weeks after all required documents are complete. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed deadline. Name approval, incorporation, regulated-activity licensing, bank accounts, payment-platform accounts, permits, and all other third-party or authority decisions remain subject to their own review and are not guaranteed by company formation.
